Who we are

An Indian export house for industrial fruit ingredients

Exotic Fruits Pvt. Ltd. is a government-recognised Star Export House. We manufacture tropical fruit pulps, purees, and concentrates in aseptic, canned, and frozen bulk formats for beverage, dairy, confectionery, and baby-food factories.

The commercial desk sits in Mumbai. Processing campuses sit next to fruit belts in Ratnagiri, Nashik, Krishnagiri, and Chittoor, so crop can move from orchard to steriliser in the same harvest window.

APEDA registration, an FSSAI Central Export Licence, and SGF Germany (IRMA) membership support shipments to Europe, the Middle East, North America, and Southeast Asia.

What we do

From belt fruit to bulk industrial pulp

We convert seasonal tropical fruit into specified factory ingredients — packed, tested, and documented for export.

Source

Direct and contract procurement from GI and commercial belts: Alphonso and Kesar mango, Totapuri, guava, papaya, pomegranate, and tomato.

Process

Receiving, washing, pulping, finishing, thermal treatment, and filling on stainless lines sized for bulk aseptic and canned output.

Specify

Each lot is released against Brix, acidity, pH, colour, and micro limits, with a batch COA for the buyer’s incoming QA.

Ship

Aseptic drums, OTS cans, and frozen bulk loaded as FCL via JNPT Mumbai and Chennai Port to the destination the contract names.

Sourcing belt

Where the fruit comes from

What makes us different

Why industrial buyers come to this desk

A starting list. Add or strike items once the commercial team confirms its own differentiators.

  1. 01

    Plants in the fruit belt

    Four campuses sit next to Alphonso, Totapuri, guava, and tomato country. That cuts haul time and keeps peak crop on the steriliser, not on the road.

  2. 02

    One spec, three pack formats

    The same industrial pulp can ship aseptic drum, OTS can, or frozen bulk, so a buyer’s filling line does not force a second supplier.

  3. 03

    Release testing in-house

    Brix, acidity, pH, colour, and micro are checked before the drum is marked for export, with a batch COA for the receiving lab.

  4. 04

    Export paperwork as a standard pack

    Star Export House, APEDA, FSSAI export licence, and SGF membership sit behind FCL documents, not as a last-minute chase.

  5. 05

    A catalogue built for factories

    Alphonso, Kesar, Totapuri, guava, papaya, pomegranate NFC, and tomato paste — specified for beverage, dairy, bakery, and infant lines, not for retail jars.
