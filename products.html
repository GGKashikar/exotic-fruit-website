Industrial products

Raw fruit pulps for global manufacturers

Bulk pulps, purees, and concentrates in aseptic, canned, and frozen formats — specified for industrial-scale manufacturing.

Bulk packaging formats

Three industrial pack types

Aseptic bag-in-drum

220 L bag-in-drum using a polyliner. Typical net 215–228 kg depending on fruit TDS.

OTS / A10 cans

3.1 kg cans · 6 per carton. Listed on mango, guava and related TDS sheets.

Frozen

1 kg × 16 or 8 kg × 2 per carton, or 200 kg bag-in-drum where specified.
