Quality & testing

Quality First

Exotic has established a detailed set of processes to ensure the end-product conforms to stringent international norms in the fruit processing industry. Every bulk lot is released against in-house laboratory parameters before dispatch to industrial processors.

QC Process Lab Parameters Certificates Quality Policy TDS Library

Assurance pathway

Quality control process

From farm MRL screening through thermal validation to batch COA release.

  1. 01

    Procurement

    Raw-material pesticide residue screening against EU AIJN, US FDA and Codex MRL frameworks before intake.

  2. 02

    Processing

    Flash sterilization and aseptic fill process validation to protect product integrity through the thermal kill step.

  3. 03

    Testing

    In-house colour, Brix, pH, viscosity, and microbial checks on sophisticated lab equipment at each processing plant.

  4. 04

    Release

    Batch Certificate of Analysis issued before dispatch so industrial buyers receive documented factory-input ingredients.

In-house QA lab

Parameters we release against

All processing plants have adequate capability for product testing. Parameters are regularly checked for conformance to established norms.

  • Colour

    Visual and instrumental colour checks against the agreed industrial specification.

  • Brix

    Soluble solids measured to confirm pulp, puree, or concentrate strength.

  • pH

    Acidity control for process stability and buyer TDS conformance.

  • Viscosity

    Bostwick and Brookefield measurements for flow behaviour on filling lines.

  • Microbial

    Microbial content checked before the lot is cleared for export dispatch.

Certifications & credentials

Quality systems behind our supply

These certifications, programmes and regulatory credentials support food safety, quality assurance and export operations for industrial fruit ingredients.

FSSC 22000

Food Safety Management System Certification

A food-safety management system certification relevant to industrial pulp, puree and concentrate manufacture.

SGF Germany / IRMA

Voluntary Control System / Approved Supplier

Participation in SGF Germany’s Voluntary Control System as an IRMA-approved supplier programme for fruit juice and related products.

HACCP

Food Safety / Hazard Control

Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points certification supporting Good Manufacturing Practices during fruit processing.

FSSAI Central Export License

Regulatory / Export Credential

Central export licence for food-business operations supplying overseas markets — a regulatory credential, not a food-safety management certification.

Quality Policy

Four principles that guide every lot

Working commitments drawn from Exotic Fruits’ Quality Policy — for safe food, responsible processing, customer focus, and continual improvement.

  1. 01

    Safe food

    Committed to the manufacture of safe food products by adhering to strict hygiene & quality norms during procurement, processing, storage and dispatch.

  2. 02

    Responsible processing

    A responsible constituent of the food chain — undertaking only those actions that improve the standards of hygiene, safety & quality in the final product.

  3. 03

    Customer focus

    Focused on the needs and sensitive to the expectations of customers; dedicated to continuous research in fruit processing, upgradation of process technologies, and development of personnel to guarantee better products.

  4. 04

    Continual improvement

    Accountable to customers for delivering quality not only in products but also in service, and committed to continual improvement of systems and processes.

Exotic Fruits Pvt. Ltd. is committed to the manufacture of safe food products by adhering to strict hygiene & quality norms during procurement, processing, storage and dispatch.

Exotic is a responsible constituent of the food chain and will undertake only those actions that improve the standards of hygiene, safety & quality in the final product.

Exotic is focused on the needs & sensitive to the expectations of customers and is dedicated to continuous research in fruit processing, upgradation of process technologies & development of its personnel to guarantee better products.

Exotic is accountable to its customers for delivering quality not only in products but also in service & is committed to continual improvement of its systems and processes.
