How raw aseptic pulps, purees and concentrates perform as factory inputs in beverage, dairy, confectionery and infant-food manufacturing lines.

Client manufacturing processes

Primary fruit ingredients standardized for global processing

Exotic Fruits supplies high-purity aseptic bases — not finished consumer SKUs — specified for industrial R&D, procurement and QA teams.

Beverage & RTD Juices

Soluble, non-separating purees that deliver consistent cloudiness, natural Brix and uniform viscosity for RTD juices, fruit nectars and functional smoothies. High-solubility mango, guava and papaya bases integrate cleanly into blending and homogenization stages without excessive pulp separation.

Dairy & Frozen Desserts

Thermally stable purees formulated as fruit-prep ingredients for cultured yogurt, ice cream ripple bases, sorbets and drinkable lassi. Controlled pH and Bostwick viscosity support pasteurization and freeze–thaw cycles without colour collapse or excessive syneresis.

Fruit Preparations Yogurt Formulations Sorbet & Ice Cream Lassi Bases
View mango dairy preps

Confectionery, Bakery & Preserves

High-solids fruit concentrates and purees designed for industrial fruit fillings, bakery preparations, jellies, gummy candies, glazes and fruit spreads. Elevated Brix grades (e.g. Totapuri concentrate, guava concentrate, papaya concentrate) reduce cook-up time and water activity in preserve lines.

Infant Food & Clean Label

Ultra-filtered, zero-additive single-strength purees released against stringent pesticide MRL, heavy metal and microbiological safety checks. Suitable as clean-label fruit bases for infant nutrition and specialty food processors requiring commercial sterility and batch COA documentation.
